# Privacy-policy wording for in-app feedback (draft, not published)

Draft paragraphs for the apps that embed OpenWish 1.2. Nothing here is live;
the owner reviews and pastes the wording into each app's privacy policy (and
checks the App Store privacy labels and the Google Play data-safety form)
before the app ships OpenWish 1.2.

## What OpenWish 1.2 sends with a report

Only when the user opens the feedback or bug-report screen and taps send:

| Data | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Title, description, optional email | typed by the user | email is only used to reply |
| Screenshots / photos | chosen by the user | up to 4, compressed |
| Selected card (card reports) | picked by the user | card id, name, set, number |
| Random feedback ID | generated by the SDK | a random UUID stored in the app; not the advertising ID, not linked to an account |
| App id, version, build | SDK | |
| OS version, device model, platform (iOS / iPadOS / macOS / Android) | SDK, new in 1.2: model, platform | e.g. `iPhone17,1`, `Pixel 9` |
| Region/locale, preferred languages, time zone | SDK, new in 1.2: languages, time zone | e.g. `de_DE`, `["de-DE","en-US"]`, `Europe/Berlin` |
| App-specific diagnostics | the app's `issueContextProvider` | see each app below |

Feature-request votes and comments send the random feedback ID and the text.
Nothing is sent in the background, and OpenWish has no analytics or tracking.

Reports are stored on our own feedback server (OpenWish, run by us on
Cloudflare: Workers, D1 database, R2 file storage, region chosen by
Cloudflare). New reports are passed to Cloudflare Workers AI in the same
Cloudflare account to create an internal English translation, a one-line
summary and a list of possibly related reports; the result is an internal note
that the user never receives. No other third party receives report content.
We reply in the app and, if the user gave an email address, by email; every
email has an unsubscribe link.

**Owner to verify before publishing:** Cloudflare's current Workers AI data
terms (customer content not used for training), the retention period you want
to state, and the controller/contact block of each policy.

## Shared paragraph (all apps)

> **Feedback and bug reports.** When you send a feature request, comment or bug
> report from the app, we receive what you enter (text, an optional email
> address, screenshots or photos you attach and, for card reports, the card you
> select) together with technical information that helps us reproduce the
> problem: app version, operating system version, device model, platform,
> language and region settings, time zone and a random feedback ID created by
> the app. These reports are stored on our own feedback server, hosted by
> Cloudflare. We use automated translation and summarisation (Cloudflare
> Workers AI) internally to read reports in every language and to find
> duplicates; this never replies to you and never decides anything about you.
> We use the data only to answer you and to fix and improve the app, and we
> delete a report on request. Your email address is optional and only used to
> reply to your report. (Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate
> interest in fixing problems you report; for the optional email Art. 6(1)(a),
> your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.)

## Per app

### Eyevo (iOS, Mac, Android)

Shared paragraph, plus:

> With a report the app also sends your purchase status (whether you have Eyevo
> Pro and the anonymous RevenueCat customer ID the app uses for purchases) and
> usage counts (scans used today, number of different cards scanned, whether the
> first scan was completed; on the Mac the library size instead), so we can
> check subscription and scan-limit problems. Card reports can include the card
> you picked from the Eyevo card database.

### Haki (iOS, Android)

Shared paragraph, plus (Android sends this today; iOS sends only the SDK
fields until the app adds a context provider):

> With a report the Android app also sends your purchase status (Haki Pro,
> active entitlements and the anonymous RevenueCat customer ID) and counts of
> scanned cards and catalog cards, so we can check subscription and scanning
> problems.

### YuScan (iOS, Android)

Shared paragraph, plus:

> With a report the app also sends your purchase status (YuScan Pro and the
> anonymous RevenueCat customer ID), scan counts (scans used today, total
> scans, scan history size), the number of cards in your collections and
> wishlist, whether the card database is ready, the scanner model version and
> which data server the app uses. Card reports can include the card you picked
> from the YuScan card database.

### Scrytics (iOS)

Shared paragraph only. The app sends no extra diagnostics with reports today.
Add a purchase-status sentence like Eyevo's if the app later attaches
RevenueCat data through `issueContextProvider`.

### Lorvo (iOS)

Shared paragraph only. The app sends no extra diagnostics with reports today.

## Store disclosures to re-check

- **App Store privacy labels:** "Diagnostics → Other Diagnostic Data" and
  "User Content → Customer Support / Photos" (user-provided), "Contact Info →
  Email Address" (optional, app functionality), "Identifiers → User ID" when the
  RevenueCat ID is attached. Not linked to identity unless the user gives an
  email; not used for tracking.
- **Google Play data safety:** "App info and performance → Diagnostics",
  "Photos", "Personal info → Email address" (optional), "App activity → Other
  user-generated content", "Device or other IDs" when the RevenueCat ID is
  attached. Data is encrypted in transit; users can request deletion.
